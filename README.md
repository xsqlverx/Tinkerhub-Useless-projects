## The Premise

We spend our days building auth flows, optimizing database queries, and tailoring code to satisfy metrics nobody actually cares about.

**Useless Projects** is an intentional detour.

It is an open registry for software, hardware, mechanical contraptions, and digital oddities that do not solve a problem, have no business model, will not secure seed funding, and will never scale.

If it works, it counts. If it barely works, it also counts. The only requirement is that you built it to see what happens when you let curiosity off its leash.

---

## Taxonomy of Artifacts

What belongs here? Generally, anything that fits into one of these categories:

### Petty Solutions

A script that scrapes a local bakery's webcam every 12 seconds to alert you the exact moment the cinnamon rolls leave the oven.

### Adversarial Interfaces

A form input that actively dodges the user's cursor, or a volume slider powered exclusively by audio pitch through the microphone.

### Pure Noise

A website that plays an ambient recording of an empty cafeteria in 2004 when you press the spacebar.
